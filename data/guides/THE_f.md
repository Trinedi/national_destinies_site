## Concept

Thessalonica is the smallest formable in the mod, holding one great city
and the Macedonian and Thessalian hinterland behind it. Do not expect an
empire.

Its identity is a single fact: **Thessalonica had three masters, and none
of them was the crown.** The Lombard baronage that Boniface of
Montferrat's knights founded in 1204. The guilds and dockhands of the
harbour, who ran the city as an independent commune for eight years. And
the Orthodox church, with the Holy Mountain behind it. Every choice in
this country comes back to that triangle.

## Forming it

1. **Be one of the Latin states of Greece.** Fifteen tags qualify. Achaea
   and the Latin Empire are explicitly excluded, and so is Albania, which
   has a formable of its own at a level this one cannot clear.
2. **Be Catholic, with a French, Iberian or Italian culture.**
3. **Own Thessaloniki.**
4. **Hold 80 percent of Macedonia and Thessaly.** Six provinces.
5. **Form Thessalonica in age 1 or age 2.**

**The age cap is the real constraint.** This formable closes when age 3
begins. If you want it, you are building for it from the opening move.

The window splits in two, and the content is built on the split. Age 1,
1337 to 1341, is the Latin inheritance: Boniface's kingdom, the baronage,
the claim. Age 2 begins in 1342, the exact year the Zealots seized
Thessalonica and began eight years of communal rule.

## Pick your founding doctrine on formation

You are choosing which of the three masters to rule through, and every
option surrenders something real.

- **Rule through the barons.** Crown estate power, garrisons, noble levies
  and demesne produce. The burghers resent it. Pushes toward aristocracy.
- **Rule through the commune.** Burgher satisfaction, urban growth, sea
  trade and sailors. You surrender your grip on your own capital and cut
  the barons out entirely. Pushes toward plutocracy.
- **Rule through the church.** Toleration of the Greek rite, devotion,
  willing Greek levies and control, at the cost of your own religion's
  tolerance, because the realm's own Latins take it as betrayal.

## What happens on formation

- Rank rises to kingdom if it was lower, preserving vanilla's own effect.
- Mild stability.

## After forming

The centrepiece is **the Zealots hold the gates**, which offers all three
historically attested exits: charter the commune and lose crown
authority; storm the lower town and hang the council, as Kantakouzenos
did in 1350, earning permanent burgher hatred; or wait, because the city
turned on the Zealots itself in 1349, at the cost of eight years of
looking helpless.

Six vanilla advances already exist here, among them the Fair of Saint
Demetrios and the University of Thessalonica. Later events cover Gregory
Palamas barred from his own see in 1347 and the Sephardic arrival of
1492. Buildings stay inside the six provinces: the Eptapyrgion citadel
whose guns face into its own city, the Sidirokausia mines, and the
Argyrokopeion mint.

Destiny takes the two ends of the same argument:

- **The barons' road.** Prosek and Melnik, then the Epirote despotate
  that destroyed the historical kingdom in 1224, then Ravennika. Capstone
  is the **Castellany of Platamon**.
- **The commune's road.** Three island ports instead of a land campaign:
  Lemnos, Negroponte and Chios. Capstone is the **House of the
  Kommerkion**.

Neither road reaches past the Aegean, and the church option has no
destiny road.

## Notes

- **Bureaucracies.** Four, of which you run two, and none is a crown
  body: every alignment is one estate approving while the other two
  resent it. The **Curia of the Lombards**, the **Archon of the Demos**
  on the waterfront, the **Synaxis of Karyes** of Athonite abbots, and
  the **Sekreton of Thessalonike**.
- That last one seats only one master instead of two, which sharpens the
  whole premise. Its decay was written by history: in 1337, the game's
  start year, three of the four judges of that tribunal were convicted of
  taking bribes and exiled.
- Boniface of Montferrat founded this kingdom in 1204 and his son
  Demetrius held it to 1224. Montferrat itself cannot form Thessalonica,
  since it is not one of the fifteen Latin tags. See the Sardinia-Piedmont
  guide for what it can do instead.
