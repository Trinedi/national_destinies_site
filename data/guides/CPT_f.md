## Concept

Coptic Egypt is a Christian minority ruling the Nile. In the early Mamluk
period the Copts were about a fifth of Egypt's people and far more than a
fifth of its administration: they ran the fiscal and scribal offices that
actually collected the revenue. A community that cannot win a battle can
still be indispensable to whoever is paying for one, and the chaos after
al-Nasir Muhammad died in 1341 gave the men who kept the books their
opening.

The window is narrow. The riots and church burnings of 1301 and 1321 were
recent memory in 1337, and conversion only accelerated afterwards. You are
building a state against the demographic trend, and everything else here
follows from that.

## Forming it

1. **Be Miaphysite.** No country starts this way with the right culture,
   so read the section below before anything else.
2. **Have Lower or Upper Egyptian primary culture.**
3. **Own Cairo, Wadi el-Natrun and Asyut.** Money, church and heartland.
   Cairo held the revenue offices and the patriarch's residence. Wadi
   el-Natrun is where the Coptic popes are chosen. Asyut is the Coptic
   heart of Upper Egypt.
4. **Hold 75 percent of Lower and Upper Egypt.**
5. **Form Coptic Egypt.** A civil war does not block you. Seizing the
   state during a collapse is the premise.

### Becoming Miaphysite

There is no Coptic religion in EU5. Copts are Miaphysite pops on
Egyptian-culture locations, thirteen of them across the two areas, so no
country can form this on day one. The road in is a confessional flip.

Play a small Egyptian-culture state and use the parliament agenda that
sets your state religion to an estate's dominant faith. That is Coptic
financiers converting fiscal weight into the state's confession, and it
is the intended route rather than a loophole.

Nubian countries cannot form it, even though Alodia and Abwab start
Miaphysite. They would have turned Coptic Egypt into a Nubian conquest.

## Pick your founding doctrine on formation

Three ways a minority regime survives.

- **Keep the ledger, change nothing else.** Mosques open, qadis in their
  courts, the state Coptic only at the top. Tax and control, bought with
  a heavy penalty to conversion speed. You promised to touch nobody's
  faith, so the clock keeps running.
- **Give the land to the patriarch.** Monastic estates to the crown, the
  Coptic calendar as the tax year, confession pressed on the countryside.
  The fastest way out of minority status, paid for in tax base and unrest.
- **Swear the oath the Bashmurites swore.** Levy size, manpower, defence
  and hostile attrition, paid in tax and reputation, because foreign
  courts read the result as a peasant rising.

## What happens on formation

- Capital moves to Cairo.
- Rank rises to kingdom.
- Mild stability only. A minority proclamation does not settle a country.
- **The dhimmi estate dissolves.** It requires a Muslim ruler. The Copts
  stop being a protected legal category and become the state, and the
  Sunni majority folds into the ordinary peasant and burgher estates.

## After forming

The demographic clock is your real opponent. The same parliament
procedure that made your confession can carry it back through those same
estates, and the arithmetic favours your enemies a little more every
decade. The age 4 advance **Confession Beyond Reversal** shuts the door
permanently in both directions: against parliament, against a patron,
against a conquering peace treaty, and against you.

Heritage runs from the diwan of the Copts and the Rawda flood gauge
through the Scala lexicons, the renewed Baqt with Nubia, the Ethiopian
Abuna, the Rawk surveys and the Coptic press.

Two destiny paths:

- **Concede the numbers.** The qadis keep their courts and the pepper
  crossing at Aydhab and Suakin pays for the state. The capstone **Bureau
  of Endowments** administers Muslim pious foundations and permanently
  stops your clergy converting anyone, so the promise binds you too.
- **Attack the numbers.** Convert at home, and annex Christian Nubia
  upriver through Qasr Ibrim and Faras to Dongola and Soba. The
  **Metropolitan See** capstone can only stand on ground that path took.

The Bashmurite doctrine has no destiny path.

## Notes

- Coptic Egypt is level 3 and Aegyptus is level 4, so you can go on to
  form Aegyptus. This is the state that precedes the imperial
  restoration, not a rival to it.
- The Bashmurites are a story being assembled rather than a living
  tradition. The last revolt was crushed in 831, Bashmur was destroyed
  and its people sold. The event asks who owns the story, the priests or
  the marsh shaykhs, not whether it is true.
- No production buildings and no ships. A riverine minority regime that
  has to buy its cavalry has no business owning the sea.
