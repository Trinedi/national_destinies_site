## Concept

The Bosporan Kingdom is a Crimean Greco-Gothic successor state
built on top of the Principality of Theodoro, the cliff-fortress
duchy at Mangup that survived from the late Roman frontier into the
1470s. The historical references are layered: the ancient Bosporan
Kingdom of the Spartocids and Mithridates VI on the Cimmerian
Strait, the Crimean Goths (the late echo of Wulfila's people, still
speaking a Germanic tongue when Ogier de Busbecq met them at the
Ottoman court in the 1560s), and the Pontic Orthodox cave
monasteries of Inkerman and Eski-Kermen.

This is Theodoro surviving Mehmed II's conquest of 1475 and hardening
into a Crimean kingdom of mixed peoples. On formation you choose which
half of that inheritance to foreground, Hellenic-Roman or Crimean
Gothic, and the choice renames the country.

## Forming it

1. **Be in the Greek culture group.** Greek, pontic_greek, griko and
   gothic all qualify.
2. **Be Orthodox.**
3. **Own Theodoro.** The cliff seat of Mangup. FEO starts with it and
   everyone else has to take it.
4. **Hold 90 percent of Crimea.** Tight enough that you need nearly
   every location on the peninsula.
5. **Form the Bosporan Kingdom.**

FEO is the intended run. TRE has the right culture and faith but sits
on the southern Black Sea coast and must project north across the
water. A BYZ run is a Byzantine restoration that swallows Crimea on
the way, playable but very long.

## Pick your founding doctrine on formation

The formation event offers two 25-year doctrines. Each renames the
country, swings a societal value, and opens one of the destiny paths.

- **Regnum Bospori.** The Hellenic-Roman reading: an heir of Eupator
  and Mithridates VI, able to meet Constantinople and Venice as a
  peer. Renames you to **Bosporus**, adjective Bosporan. Pushes you
  toward innovative. Opens the Heir of Mithridates path.
- **Cimmerian Bosporus.** The Crimean Gothic reading: a mountain
  kingdom of mixed peoples bound by Orthodox faith and the iron of
  Mangup. Renames you to the **Cimmerian Bosporus**, adjective
  Cimmerian. Pushes you toward quality. Opens the Ecumenical Throne
  path.

## What happens on formation

- Mild stability bonus.
- Country rank rises to Kingdom if you are Duchy or below.
- Your doctrine renames the country.

## After forming

Tier 1 heritage covers the principality's foundational identity:
the Mangup Citadel (cliff fortress), the Komnenoid (Gabras) dynastic
claim, the Tauric emporion (Cherson, Caffa, Soldaia trade), the
Crimean Gothic tongue, and the cave monasteries. Each comes with a
DHE and a 15-year modifier choice.

The destiny tree splits two ways:

- **Path A: Heir of Mithridates.** Black Sea hegemony. Stage 1 takes
  the southern Pontic ports of Sinope and Amasra. Stage 2 takes
  Trebizond and Varna for Black Sea Mastery. Stage 3 takes
  Constantinople and Burgas and unlocks the **Mithridatic
  Admiralty**. Requires the Regnum Bospori choice.
- **Path B: Ecumenical Throne.** A steppe and Rus' empire of many
  peoples. Stage 1 takes Tana and Sarai al-Jadid, the Don and Volga
  gateways. Stage 2 reaches into Kyiv. Stage 3 takes Beljamen, Dymer
  and Tokat and unlocks the **Imperial Chancery**. Requires the
  Cimmerian Bosporus choice.

## Notes

- The 90 percent Crimea requirement is tight. Losing one location to
  a Tatar or Genoese raid blocks formation until you take it back.
- Both doctrines rename the country, so your ledger tag and your
  displayed name will stop matching. You remain TDR underneath.
- The two paths pull hard in opposite directions. Mithridates is a
  naval push south toward Constantinople; the Ecumenical Throne is a
  land push north toward Kyiv. Choose at formation with that in mind.
