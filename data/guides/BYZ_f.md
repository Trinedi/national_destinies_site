special_notes:
  - kind: warn
    title: "If you start as Byzantium"
    body: >
      A country that begins the game as Byzantium cannot use the formable
      button; you cannot form what you already are. To unlock the formation
      event, its timed modifier, and the destiny tree, reconquer the three-city
      Komnenian core: **Constantinople, Athens, and Iznik** (you start owning
      only Constantinople) while following either **Orthodoxy** or the restored
      **Hellenic** faith (the old Roman religion, via Fate of the Phoenix). The
      formation event adapts its choices to your religion. The check runs every
      four years, so formation fires on the next tick once all three are held.
    locations: [constantinople, athens, iznik]
