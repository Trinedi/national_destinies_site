    title: "Moving the crown to Brazil"
    body: >
      As Portugal you can carry the crown across the Atlantic. The decision
      **Send the Court to Rio** opens once Lisbon is your capital, Rio de
      Janeiro is a town, all of its province is settled and a quarter of the
      Pindorama area is yours or your subjects'. It starts **the
      Transmigration**, and a move that succeeds lets you form **Brazil**. Only
      a player can take this road. See the Brazil page for the full checklist.
    locations: [lisbon, goana_para]
