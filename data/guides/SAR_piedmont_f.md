## Concept

Sardinia-Piedmont is a crown assembled by treaty out of an Alpine duchy
and a Mediterranean island, holding the passes into Italy and governing
two populations under two legal traditions. The historical Kingdom of
Sardinia exists because Victor Amadeus II swapped Sicily for Sardinia at
The Hague in 1720, having been handed Sicily at Utrecht seven years
earlier.

Its formation event asks what a crown assembled that way actually rests
on. Geography, law, or descent.

## Forming it

1. **Have an Italian culture.** Any culture in the Italian group. That is
   the only requirement on who you are.
2. **Hold 85 percent of Sardinia and Piedmont.** A high fraction across
   two areas that do not touch.
3. **Form Sardinia-Piedmont.**

There is no city and no rank requirement. Sardinia-Piedmont is level 2
and chains into Italy at level 3, which is historically exact, since this
is the state that actually unified Italy.

### The Montferrat fork

Playing Montferrat, vanilla fires an event that decides your campaign.
The marquisate has been ruled by the Palaiologos since 1306, and the
event asks whether to keep the Latin, Piedmontese identity your ancestor
adopted or reclaim the Greek one. Stay Piedmontese and this formable
stays open, with Italy beyond it. Take the Byzantine court and you become
Greek and Orthodox, permanently shut out of Sardinia-Piedmont and
Thessalonica, but able to form **Byzantium** instead.

## Pick your founding doctrine on formation

- **Hold the doors of Italy.** Geography as the instrument: movement,
  supply depots, foraging, road building and defensiveness, bought with
  diplomatic reputation, because a state that lives off tolls behind a
  wall is not trusted.
- **Two codes, one registry, one crown.** Law as the instrument:
  legislative efficiency, integration, assimilation, cultures capacity and
  heretic tolerance, bought with noble satisfaction, because codification
  takes the barons' courts away.
- **Rule as the purple ruled.** Montferrat only. Prestige, diplomatic
  reputation, cultural tradition, relations and legitimacy, bought with
  cabinet efficiency, because a court that runs on descent and ceremony
  keeps fewer clerks.

## What happens on formation

- Rank rises to kingdom, but only if you are not a member of the Holy
  Roman Empire. This preserves vanilla's own behaviour exactly.

## After forming

The heritage tree runs 31 advances across both halves of the realm, and
its best material is the island's. **Eleanor of Arborea's Carta de Logu**
of 1392 is 198 articles in Sardinian, setting one penalty regardless of
rank, protecting widows' property, and regulating forest and livestock.
It remained Sardinia's law until 1827, and the age 6 advance closes that
bracket with the Codice Feliciano.

Alongside it: the giudicati courts, the Cagliari saltpans, the alpine
tolls of Mont Cenis, Turin becoming the capital in 1563, and Fenestrelle.
The Waldensians of the Piedmontese valleys survived, unlike the Cathars
of Occitania, and their levy raises only from Vivaro-Alpine speakers.

Destiny continues the two non-Montferrat answers:

- **The passes.** Briancon and Martigny, then a quay of your own at Nice
  and Savona, then Geneva and Genoa. None is Savoyard at the start.
  Capstone is the **Ospizio del Valico**, a pass-summit hospice built on
  mountains with a road.
- **The registry.** Two of its three stages ask for institutions rather
  than armies, and its targets are law schools: Pavia, then Bastia and
  Corte, then Bologna and Padua. Capstone is the **Archivio di Corte**,
  raised at Turin in 1731.

## Notes

- **Bureaucracies.** Four, of which you run two, and two of them are
  opposed. The **intendenti**, Victor Amadeus II's provincial intendants
  who broke local and feudal jurisdiction, make revoking privilege cheap.
  The **Order of Saints Maurice and Lazarus**, from the bull of 1572,
  makes granting it cheap. Choosing between them is political rather than
  additive. The others are the **Regio Economato**, the crown
  administration of vacant benefices under the concordat of 1727, which
  carries the right to ignore papal bulls, and the **Magistrato della
  Riforma**, which licensed the kingdom's schools.
- Nothing here duplicates the separate Sardinia formable: no Carabinieri,
  no Risorgimento, no Cavour, no Nuraghi, no Stamenti. And where Italy
  owns the urban setificio, this tag takes the rural water-powered
  throwing mill instead.
