## Concept

Illyria is a Catholic Slavic frontier crown on the eastern Adriatic. Its
export is priests rather than goods, its border is a permanent militarised
cordon, and its economy is portage between the Pannonian interior and the
sea.

The name here is the older one, not Napoleon's Illyrian Provinces of 1809
or the Illyrian Movement of the 1830s, both of which fall outside this
game. Illyrian was the learned and ecclesiastical name the Catholic Slavs
of the eastern Adriatic gave themselves, roughly 1450 to 1650. Vinko
Pribojevic of Hvar delivered an oration on it in 1525, Mavro Orbini of
Ragusa published *Il Regno degli Slavi* in 1601, and Gregory XIII founded
the Illyrian College at Loreto in 1580 to train Catholic clergy for Slavic
lands under Ottoman rule.

## Forming it

1. **Have Croatian or Dalmatian primary culture.**
2. **Be Catholic.** If you intend to reform, form first. A Protestant
   Croatia loses access, because the Illyrian claim in this period is
   Roman all the way down.
3. **Own Split and Zagreb.** Split starts Venetian with a Croatian core
   on it. Zagreb is the seat of Slavonia.
4. **Hold 60 percent of Croatia, Slavonia and Bosnia.** The fraction is
   low because Bosnia is Ottoman-contested for most of the game.
5. **Form Illyria.**

Those two cities are the whole campaign in miniature: take the Dalmatian
coast back off Venice, then absorb the banate.

Illyria sits at level 3, which is what lets Croatia reach it at all.
Croatia's own formable is level 2, and a country can only form something
above its own level. A formed Dalmatia and the Dalmatian communes qualify
as well.

## Pick your founding doctrine on formation

Split has just been taken off Venice and Zagreb's two feuding quarters are
being told to be one city. The axis is the wall or the mission.

- **Confinia Regni.** Garrison size, hostile attrition, levy size, supply
  limit and army tradition, costing heathen tolerance, which bites
  precisely because this realm holds Bosnia.
- **Sacrum Illyricum.** Heathen conversion, clergy literacy, institution
  growth, papal relations and literacy, costing manpower, because
  seminaries are not regiments.

## What happens on formation

- Rank rises to kingdom if it was lower. Croatia is already one, so this
  matters on the Ragusan and communal routes.
- Mild stability.

## After forming

The heritage tree runs to 33 advances, and the best thing in it comes
first: **the Glagolitic liturgy**. Innocent IV's grant of 1248 to the
bishop of Senj permitted Mass in Slavonic in Glagolitic letters, justified
by the false attribution of the script to St Jerome. That attribution is
why the Roman institution is San *Girolamo*. A Catholic state with its own
vernacular liturgy in a non-Latin alphabet is unique in Latin Christendom.

After that: the Vinodol Code of 1288, the Senj press of 1494, Marulic's
*Judita* of 1501, the Military Frontier of 1553, and the recovery of
Slavonia in 1699.

Destiny follows your formation choice:

- **Confinium.** The cordon stops being a line to hold and becomes a line
  to move, following the Croatian and Slavonian generalcies after 1683:
  across the Drava, then Belgrade, then the Banat. Capstone is the
  **Frontier Council**, your own war council instead of Graz's.
- **Sacrum Illyricum continued.** Institutions, presses and endowments,
  and two of its three stages have no location objective at all. Capstone
  is the **Marian Shrine**, which must stand on a hill: Trsat, Olovo or
  Bistrica.

## Notes

- **Bureaucracies.** Four, of which you run two. The standout is the
  **loca credibilia**, the places of authentic faith: cathedral chapters
  and monastic convents held public notarial authority, issuing and
  authenticating legal instruments as an arm of the state. The others are
  the **Sabor**, which elected a king at Cetin on 1 January 1527, the
  **canonical visitations**, whose 214 volumes of parish protocols stand
  in for a census, and the **Tabula Banalis** of 1723.
- **No ships.** The uskoks, the argosies and the Adriatic navy belong to
  Dalmatia. Illyria is the inland consolidation of that world.
- Three modifier keys here are held by no other country in the mod:
  clergy rural desired pop, clergy desired pop, and heathen pop
  conversion speed. A state whose export is priests measures differently
  from its neighbours.
