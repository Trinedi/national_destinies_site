## Concept

Illyria is a Catholic Slavic clerical-frontier crown on the eastern
Adriatic. Its export is priests rather than goods, its border is a
permanent militarised cordon, and its economy is portage between the
Pannonian interior and the sea.

**Read this part before anything else, because the obvious reading of the
name is the wrong one for this period.** The famous Illyria is the
Napoleonic Illyrian Provinces of 1809 and the Illyrian Movement of the
1830s, and neither is in this game's window. Neither appears anywhere in
this content.

What is period-correct, and what this country is built on, is
"Illyrian" as the learned and ecclesiastical self-designation of the
Catholic Slavs of the eastern Adriatic, roughly 1450 to 1650. Vinko
Pribojevic of Hvar delivered an oration on it in 1525. Mavro Orbini of
Ragusa published *Il Regno degli Slavi* in 1601. Rome had the church and
college of San Girolamo degli Illirici from 1453, rebuilt under Sixtus V
in 1589, and Gregory XIII founded the Illyrian College at Loreto in 1580
to train Catholic clergy specifically for Slavic lands under Ottoman
rule. This is a humanist and clerical identity, not an ethnic-nationalist
federation.

One strand is deliberately absent. The Habsburgs did later use "Illyrian"
as a formal administrative category (*Gens Illyrica*, in the charters of
1706, 1713 and 1715), but that category covered the **Orthodox Serb**
settlers of southern Hungary under Leopold's 1690 privileges, not
Catholic Croats. Building on it would have produced a Habsburg Serb
entity that duplicates Serbia.

## Forming it

**Requirements.** Croatian or Dalmatian primary culture, and Catholic
religion. You must own **Split and Zagreb**. Territory is Croatia,
Slavonia and Bosnia at 60 percent.

The fraction leans low because Bosnia is Ottoman-contested for most of
the game.

**The Catholic gate is an identity gate, not an oversight.** A Croatian
country that goes Protestant loses access, because the entire Illyrian
claim in this period is Roman. If you intend to reform, form first.

The two cities are the whole route in miniature: Split is Venetian at the
start with a Croatian core on it, and Zagreb is Slavonia's capital. So
forming Illyria means taking the Dalmatian cities back from Venice and
absorbing the banate, which is precisely the grievance the identity was
built out of.

## The level, and why it is 3

Illyria is **level 3**, not level 2. Vanilla's Croatia formable is level
2, and the engine only permits forming a country of strictly higher
level, so a level 2 Illyria would have been invisible to Croatia, the one
country it exists for.

This is exactly the trap that made Dalmatia unformable by Croatia in the
first place, which is what the requesters were complaining about. Level 3
also keeps a formed Dalmatia (level 1) and the Dalmatian communes as
valid origins.

## Pick your founding doctrine on formation

Split has just been taken off Venice and Zagreb's two feuding quarters
are being told to be one city. The axis is **the wall or the mission**.

- **Confinia Regni.** Garrison size, hostile attrition, levy size, supply
  limit and army tradition, with a real cost in heathen tolerance that
  bites precisely because this realm holds Bosnia. Note it is
  deliberately **not** named Antemurale Christianitatis: the Danubian
  Monarchy already ships a formation option under that exact name, so the
  papal compliment lives in the age 3 advance instead.
- **Sacrum Illyricum.** Heathen conversion, clergy literacy, institution
  growth, papal relations and literacy, costing manpower, because
  seminaries are not regiments.

## What happens on formation

Rank rises to kingdom if it was lower, which is a no-op for Croatia (it
already starts a kingdom) and meaningful on the Ragusan and communal
routes. Mild stability.

## After forming

The heritage tree is the densest in this batch at 33 advances, and the
best single thing in it is first: **the Glagolitic liturgy**. Innocent
IV's 1248 grant to the bishop of Senj permitted Mass in the Slavonic
tongue in Glagolitic letters, justified by the false attribution of the
script to St Jerome. That attribution is the reason the Roman institution
is San *Girolamo*. A Catholic state with its own vernacular liturgy in a
non-Latin alphabet is genuinely unique in Latin Christendom, and the
whole Illyrian claim hangs off it.

From there: the Vinodol Code of 1288, the Tricesima customs, the Senj
press of 1494, Bosna Srebrena and the Milodraz charter, the chapter walls,
Marulic's *Judita* of 1501, the haramije, the Military Frontier of 1553,
Karlstadt, the Illyrian colleges, the Illyrian grammars of Vrancic and
Kasic, the Croat regiments, the Magnates' Conspiracy, the recovery of
Slavonia in 1699, the Carolina road and Stulli's lexicon.

Destiny splits along the formation choice:

- **Confinium.** The cordon stops being a line to hold and becomes a line
  to move, following what the Croatian and Slavonian generalcies actually
  did after 1683: across the Drava, then Belgrade, then the Banat.
  Capstone is the **Frontier Council**, because a kingdom that carried the
  line to the Banat out of its own treasury keeps its own war council
  instead of Graz's. The mid-path choice is the Statuta Valachorum of
  1630, a chartered frontier against a commissioned command.
- **Sacrum Illyricum continued.** Institutions, presses and endowments
  first: two of its three stages have no location objective at all. The
  Congregation of Propaganda, then the Union of Marca read either as a
  settlement where the Vlachs keep their rite or as a waystation to Latin
  bishops, then the Illyrian Church. Capstone is the **Marian Shrine**,
  gated to hill locations (Trsat above the Rijeka crossing, Olovo in the
  Bosnian hills, Bistrica in the Zagorje).

Neither path touches Kosovo, Raska, Zeta or Sumadija beyond Belgrade and
Smederevo, so the tree never reads as South Slav unification.

## Notes

**Bureaucracies.** Four, of which you run two, and the standout is the
**loca credibilia**, the "places of authentic faith". In the Hungarian and
Croatian lands, cathedral chapters and monastic convents held public
notarial authority, issuing and authenticating legal instruments as an
arm of the state. A church body performing a state registry function is a
genuinely unusual arrangement and it fits a clerical crown exactly. The
others are the **Sabor** (the congregation of the realm, which elected a
king at Cetin on 1 January 1527), the **canonical visitations** (the
archdeacon's circuit from 1615, 214 volumes of parish protocols, the
kingdom's substitute for a census), and the **Tabula Banalis** of 1723.

**No ships, deliberately.** The uskoks, the argosies and the Adriatic navy
belong to Dalmatia. Illyria is the inland consolidation of that world, not
a bigger copy of it.

**Three modifier keys in this tag are held by no other country in the
mod:** clergy rural desired pop, clergy desired pop, and heathen pop
conversion speed. A state whose export is priests measures differently
from everything around it.
