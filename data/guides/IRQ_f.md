## Concept

Iraq restores the Sawad, the irrigated land between the Tigris and the
Euphrates that once yielded roughly a fifth of the revenue of the entire
Caliphate, as a sovereign Muslim state ruled from Baghdad.

The important thing to understand is that this is not a dead-empire
revival. The Jalayirids kept a real court in Baghdad through the 1330s
and beyond. What died was not the polity but the water. The Nahrawan
canal was breached during a civil war in 937 and never properly
repaired, and that collapse, rather than the Mongol sack of 1258, is the
concrete reason Mesopotamia stopped being the richest tax province in
the Islamic world and became a border march of whoever held Tabriz,
Cairo or Istanbul.

So the tag's whole spine is water. Not "economy" in the abstract:
specific canals, specific weirs, and a tax system that makes the
treasury care whether they silt up.

## Forming it

**Requirements.** Muslim religion group, and either an `arabic_group`
primary culture or a capital inside Iraq al-Arabi. Your capital must sit
inside the required territory. You must own **Baghdad, Basra and Kufa**,
and not be in a civil war. Territory is Iraq al-Arabi plus the Jazira at
50 percent.

The fraction is deliberately permissive because Iraq al-Arabi alone is
56 locations across nine provinces. Consolidating the south is enough to
clear the bar, which keeps Iraq a lower-Mesopotamian state. The Jazira
counts toward the total but is not a hard requirement, so the upper
country is claimable rather than compulsory.

The three required cities are all in the south and are the point: the
capital, the head of the Gulf, and the shrine country.

## Pick your founding doctrine on formation

Al-Mansur's city was two things at once, and they did not fall together.
The choice is which one you are rebuilding, and it is not a military
versus economic split.

- **Dar al-Khilafa, the House of the Caliphate.** Baghdad as the
  political capital of the Muslim world: prestige, legitimacy, control
  and holy-war war score. It carries a real cost in reduced heretic
  tolerance, because a Sunni caliphal restoration reads as a threat in
  Najaf and Hilla, which are inside your own borders.
- **Madinat al-Salam, the City of Peace.** Baghdad as the civilizational
  capital: research, literacy and institutions, with increased heathen
  tolerance because the translation movement ran on Christian, Sabian
  and Jewish scholars. It costs manpower, because the scholar-city does
  not fill its barracks.

## What happens on formation

Your capital moves to Baghdad. Your primary culture becomes Iraqi (this
is an outcome of formation rather than a gate on it, because no country
on the 1337 map starts with Iraqi as its primary culture). Your rank
rises to kingdom if it was lower, restoring the Jalayirid sultanate's
standing.

**Your pops are deliberately left alone.** Unlike the Syriac chain,
forming Iraq converts nothing. Mesopotamia stayed plural through this
entire period, with Kurdish, Turkoman, Syriac and Mandean communities
alongside the Arab majority, and homogenising it by decree would be both
bad history and a free lunch.

## After forming

The heritage tree runs on the canals. Age 1 opens the Nahrawan
head-works. Age 3 replaces fixed-area assessment with muqasama
crop-share, which taxes land according to how the water is lifted, at
half rate for flood-fed ground, a third under a wheel and a quarter
under an animal. That is the mechanism that makes the treasury lose
money when a canal silts, which is the only arrangement that ever made a
state dredge. Age 5 is the Hindiyya channel and a genuinely zero-sum
water dispute: letting the new Najaf channel run kills the Hilla branch,
or you build the regulator at the fork and pay for it in minted coin.

The destiny tree splits along the formation choice:

- **Dar al-Khilafa.** The caliphal road. Take the Zagros gates at
  Kermanshah and Hamadan, then the Syrian wing at Aleppo and Damascus,
  then Cairo and Jerusalem to seat a shadow caliph. Capstone building is
  the **Dar al-Barid**, the caliphal post relay with a resident
  intelligencer reporting on the governor next door.
- **Madinat al-Salam.** Water as machinery, which is the part of Iraq's
  hydraulic identity the heritage tree never touches. Maragheh's
  observatory instruments, then Shushtar's Sasanian weirs, then the
  corrected astronomical tables at Samarkand. Capstones are the **Dulab
  Works**, a yard building and repairing norias and al-Jazari pumps, and
  the **Marsad** observatory, which is cities-only and Crescent-only
  because a zij is written for one latitude.

The destiny root, **The Register of Waters**, is a standing roll of every
offtake, weir and sluice, walked yearly and dated. The heritage tree digs
on emergency three times and never leaves an office behind; the root is
the office.

## Notes

**Bureaucracies.** Four, of which you can run two. The **mirab and the
nawba** administer the timed water turn and call the yearly canal
clearance. The **soyurghal** is the Mongol grant in perpetuity inherited
from the Jalayirids, which hardened into hereditary tax-immune holdings.
Those two argue with each other on purpose: the mirab raises your rural
control ceiling and a neglected soyurghal collapses it, which is the
historical mechanism (the assignee standing between the treasury and the
ground is why nobody dredged) expressed as a choice between two slots.
The other two are the **jahbadhiyya**, the Jewish court bankers who made
monthly advances against Ahvaz revenue, and the **naqib al-ashraf**, the
register of the Prophet's descendants.

**Iraq chains upward into the Khilafah (KHL, level 5)**, which is the
natural continuation of the Dar al-Khilafa path. Note again that a Shia
Iraq cannot take that chain, because Khilafah gates on Sunni.

**Deliberately not here:** the Bayt al-Hikma, the Hajj and the caliphate
itself all belong to Khilafah, so Iraq reaches for the **Mustansiriya**
instead, a real building that is still standing, gated to Baghdad and
nowhere else. The Syriac Christian chain (Beth Nahrain and the Assyrian
Empire) shares the Jazira as counted territory but nothing else: it is
gated on Miaphysite or Nestorian religion, so no country ever chooses
between Iraq and Assyria.
