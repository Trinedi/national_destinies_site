## Concept

Outremer is the crusader capstone: the tier that the Kingdom of
Jerusalem, the Latin Empire, Crusader Africa, the Knights of the Temple
and the Sovereign Order of Saint John all feed into.

It is not about conquest. The defining fact of the crusader states was
that a tiny Frankish ruling caste governed an overwhelmingly non-Latin
population: Muslim, Eastern Christian, Jewish and Samaritan. This is a
country about governing people of other faiths, and about deciding whether
to leave them in it.

## Forming it

1. **Be Catholic.**
2. **Be a kingdom or an empire**, or have held Jerusalem, the Latin
   Empire, Crusader Africa, the Knights of the Temple or the Sovereign
   Order of Saint John.
3. **Own Jerusalem, Acre, Cairo, Baghdad, Mosul, Mecca, Medina and Adan.**
   Acre was the kingdom's capital from 1191 and its last mainland holding
   in 1291.
4. **Hold 60 percent of the Crescent and Egypt, plus the Hijaz, South
   Yemen and Cyprus.** Roughly 278 of 462 locations.
5. **Be at peace with yourself.** A civil war blocks formation.
6. **Form Outremer.**

**Tolerate or convert: the law The Faiths of the East.** Every Catholic
country holding land in the Latin East has it, so you choose long before
forming. It acts on those lands only.

- **Customs of the Land.** As the crusader states did: commoners there
  cannot convert, tolerance of other faiths rises, the Latin clergy
  resents it.
- **Union with Rome.** The Eastern churches join Rome, as the Maronites
  did in 1182: Eastern Christians convert faster, Muslims slower.
- **Latin Mission.** The friars preach to all: faster conversion, less
  tolerance of every other faith.

Since each estate's satisfaction follows the crown's tolerance of its
faith, a realm that keeps its many faiths lives on its tolerance of
heathens and heretics.

## Pick your founding doctrine on formation

The formation event is a coronation that inherits a problem. The herald
reads out the style, then the chamberlain hands over the survey: the
barons' court judges the Franks and almost no one else, forty frontier
castles answer to Rome, and your army is paid for by the poll tax on
subjects who are not Christian.

- **The Compact of the Courts.** Tolerance, cultures capacity,
  separatism, control and estate satisfaction, bought with a heavy cut to
  crown estate power, because you have surrendered jurisdiction.
- **Custodian of the Sanctuaries.** Red Sea trade, tax, trade centre
  power, merchant capacity and supply, bought with your own religion's
  tolerance, because your own clergy call it apostasy.
- **The New Towns.** Construction, building efficiency, population
  growth, development and assimilation, bought with separatism, because
  settlers are planted among people who did not ask for them.

## What happens on formation

- Capital moves to Jerusalem.
- Rank rises to empire.
- Large stability bonus.

## After forming

Heritage is built out of institutions rather than armies: the *ra'is*
village headmen through whom Frankish lords administered non-Latin
communities, the capitation inherited from the Fatimid diwan, the
Maronite crossbow muster, and the absence of serfdom.

Three of its thirty advances carry a permanent downside. The military
orders' castellany gives you the best fortified frontier in the world and
costs crown power forever; the Italian communes give you trade and cost
tax revenue forever.

Destiny splits into the compact and the sea:

- **The Compact of the Courts** continues into development, institutions
  and jurisdiction, ending at a **Chartered Quarter**, the Venetian and
  Genoese instrument issued by charter instead of extracted by a fleet.
- **Custodian of the Sanctuaries** runs the sea that the pilgrimage and
  the pepper both cross: Aydhab and Suakin, then Hormuz and Muscat, then
  Calicut and Diu. Capstone is the **Granary of the Two Cities**, the
  Egyptian grain endowment that fed Mecca and Medina under four dynasties
  and returned nothing to any of them.

## Notes

- **Bureaucracies.** Six, of which you run three, and they are six
  positions in one argument about who owns the ports and the courts: the
  **Court of the Syrians**, the **Court of the Chain** at the harbour,
  the **Charters of the Communes**, the **Office of the Turcopolier**
  commanding native light horse, the **New Towns**, and the **Restor of
  the Host**, the crown's duty to replace horses lost in service.
- The Turcopolier carries the tag's sharpest modifier, levy size from pops
  of non-accepted culture. Outremer's military problem as one number.
- The New Towns bureaucracy needs the New Towns formation option, which
  has no other follow-through. Take it and you see six institutions for
  three slots. Otherwise five.
