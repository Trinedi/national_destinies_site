## Concept

Outremer is the crusader **capstone**: the tier that the Kingdom of
Jerusalem, the Latin Empire, Crusader Africa, the Knights of the Temple
and the Sovereign Order of Saint John all feed into. It is not a sixth
parallel crusader tag.

The important thing is what it is *about*, which is not conquest. The
distinctive fact of the crusader states was that a tiny Frankish ruling
caste governed an overwhelmingly non-Latin population: Muslim, Eastern
Christian, Jewish and Samaritan. Outremer is that problem made
mechanical. It is a country about governing a majority you cannot convert
and must not try to.

That is enforced rather than narrated, and it is worth being explicit
about how.

## The clause that defines the country

Outremer's own formation requirement caps Catholics at **50 percent or
less of your own population.**

You may only proclaim it while your own church is a minority among your
own subjects. Nothing else in the mod uses this shape. It does three
things at once:

1. It hard-blocks the convert-or-depopulate route to a "clean" crusader
   empire.
2. It counts Eastern Christians, Jews and Samaritans on the governed
   side, not just Muslims.
3. It deliberately excludes a Catholic-majority world empire. A France or
   Castile that keeps its metropole and also takes the Middle East cannot
   form this, and that is correct: the country is named for being
   overseas. France with a Levantine province is France.

In practice it is easy to satisfy for the intended player, because
anyone holding 278 locations across the Crescent, Egypt and the Hijaz is
governing an overwhelmingly non-Catholic population. It only bites
someone dragging a large Catholic metropole behind them.

The consequence for play: **this is the one Catholic country in the game
that cannot use the Catholic religion tree's real content.** There is not
one discipline bonus, not one conversion-speed modifier and not one
holy-war-score line in the entire tag, across heritage, destiny and
bureaucracies. Instead, because each estate's satisfaction and power are
set by the crown's tolerance of *that estate's* faith, heathen and
heretic tolerance are simply this country's stability. They are the core
stat, not decoration.

## Forming it

**Requirements.** Catholic, plus either kingdom or empire rank or having
held one of the five feeder tags, plus the minority clause above.

You must own **Jerusalem, Acre, Cairo, Baghdad, Mosul, Mecca, Medina and
Adan**. Territory is the Crescent and Egypt regions plus the Hijaz and
South Yemen areas and Cyprus, at 60 percent, which is roughly 278 of 462
locations.

Acre is not decorative: it was the kingdom's actual capital from 1191 and
its last mainland holding in 1291.

Persia is deliberately excluded despite being in the original proposal.
Persia was the Ilkhanate's half of the Franco-Mongol scheme and never a
Frankish war aim. North Yemen is excluded too: highland Zaydi country was
never a Red Sea-mouth objective.

Every required city was a real Frankish war aim. Cairo was assaulted at
Damietta in 1219 and 1249, and the Franks were a march from it in 1167.
Baghdad and Mosul fell in 1258 to a Mongol army that contained Bohemond
VI of Antioch and Hethum I of Armenia. Raynald of Chatillon's Red Sea
flotilla of 1182 landed a party between Mecca and Medina. Albuquerque
assaulted Aden in 1513 as Portuguese state policy.

## Pick your founding doctrine on formation

The formation event is a coronation that inherits a problem, and it is
written that way. The herald reads the style out, then the chamberlain
hands over the survey: fewer than two in ten hear Mass in Latin, the
Haute Cour has jurisdiction over almost none of them, forty frontier
castles belong to orders answering to Rome, the Italian quarters in your
own ports keep their own courts and gallows and pay nothing, and the
revenue funding your field army is the poll tax on subjects who are not
Christian, paid in exchange for your protection.

- **The Compact of the Courts.** Tolerance, cultures capacity,
  separatism, control and estate satisfaction, bought with a heavy cut to
  crown estate power, because you have surrendered jurisdiction.
- **Custodian of the Sanctuaries.** Red Sea trade, tax, trade-centre
  power, merchant capacity, supply and reputation, bought with your own
  religion's tolerance, because your own clergy call it apostasy.
- **The New Towns.** Construction, building efficiency, population growth,
  development and assimilation, bought with separatism, because settlers
  are planted among people who did not ask for them.

## What happens on formation

Capital to Jerusalem, rank to empire.

## After forming

The heritage tree is built from institutions rather than armies: the
*ra'is* village headmen through whom Frankish lords actually
administered non-Latin communities, the capitation inherited from the
Fatimid diwan, the Maronite crossbow muster, and the fact that the
crusader states introduced **no serfdom** (native peasants owed rent
rather than labour dues, in places as little as a quarter of the crop).

Three of its thirty advances carry a **permanent** downside, which is
unusual for this mod and is the tag's tone. The military orders'
castellany gives you the best fortified frontier in the world and costs
crown power forever. The Italian communes give you trade and merchant
capacity and cost tax revenue forever. Abolishing the capitation costs
the revenue that funded the field army.

Destiny splits into the compact and the sea:

- **The Compact of the Courts** continues into development, institutions
  and jurisdiction, ending at a **Chartered Quarter**: the Venetian and
  Genoese instrument issued by charter instead of extracted by a fleet.
  Its stage 1 target is Cilicia, because Smbat Sparapet's 1265 Armenian
  translation is the only surviving text of the Assises of Antioch. Its
  stage 3 target is Sultaniyya, because the Latin archbishopric erected
  there in 1318 held a nominal province covering all Asia, which is where
  a register of chartered communities logically ends.
- **Custodian of the Sanctuaries** runs the sea the pilgrimage and the
  pepper both cross: Aydhab and Suakin, then Hormuz and Muscat, then
  Calicut and Diu. Capstone is the **Granary of the Two Cities**, the
  Egyptian grain endowment that fed Mecca and Medina under four dynasties
  and returned nothing to any of them.

## Notes

**Bureaucracies.** Six, of which you run three, and they are six
positions in the same argument about who owns the ports and the courts.
The **Court of the Syrians** under the ra'is, sitting beside the qadi and
the bet din. The **Court of the Chain**, the harbour court at the catena.
The **Charters of the Communes**, from the Pactum Warmundi of 1123, which
give the Italian quarters their own court and gallows. The **Office of
the Turcopolier**, commanding native light horse. The **New Towns**, from
Magna Mahumeria where 92 burgesses were sworn in 1156. And the **Restor
of the Host**, the crown's obligation to replace horses lost in service.

Two of them charge you permanently even while working perfectly: the
communes hold burgher power unconditionally and still lower your urban
control ceiling in your own ports.

The Turcopolier carries the tag's sharpest single modifier, **levy size
from pops of non-accepted culture**. That is Outremer's military problem
stated as one number, and no other file in the mod uses it.

**The New Towns bureaucracy is gated on having taken the New Towns
formation option**, which otherwise has no follow-through, since destiny
supports only two branches. If you take that option you see six
institutions for three slots; otherwise five.

**Overlaps that never matter.** Outremer shares required cities with
Aegyptus (Cairo, Mecca) and the Assyrian Empire (Cairo, Baghdad, Mosul),
but all three are religiously exclusive, so no country ever chooses
between them.
