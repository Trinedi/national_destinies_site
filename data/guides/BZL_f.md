## Concept

In 1807 the Portuguese court fled Napoleon and ruled from Rio de Janeiro
for fourteen years. Here you make that move yourself: you carry the crown
across the Atlantic, office by office, and build a kingdom in Brazil that
can outgrow the one in Lisbon.

## Forming it

1. **Play Portugal, with Lisbon as your capital.**
2. **Settle all of Nheteroia,** Rio de Janeiro's province, yourself or
   through your colony, with no charter still running there.
3. **Grow Rio de Janeiro into a town.**
4. **Settle a quarter of the Pindorama area** with your own or your
   subjects' land.
5. **Take the decision Send the Court to Rio.** Rio becomes your capital
   and the Transmigration begins.
6. **Move the Royal Council, the Supreme Court and the Royal Treasury to
   Rio,** with a quarter of your nobles and 15% of your clergy in the
   Americas. After that the court cannot go back.
7. **End the move with Two Crowns or Portugal Let Go,** then form Brazil.

## Pick your founding doctrine on formation

- **A crown of two shores** (30 years): reputation, control and prestige.
  Opens the court path of the destiny tree.
- **The New World first** (30 years): colonists, migration and growth.
  Opens the frontier path.

## What happens on formation

Brazil becomes a kingdom if it is not one yet and gains stability. For ten
years its population grows faster.

## After forming

- **The Crown in Rio**, your destiny tree. The court path runs through
  The Garden of Cayenne, A Court of the Arts and A Court Among the Powers.
  The frontier path runs through Colonia do Sacramento, The Captaincy of
  Mato Grosso and The Cisplatine Province.
- **Five royal offices of your own**, among them The King's Couples, which
  brings settler families to empty land.
- **Keep Brazil growing** with the cabinet action Send Settlers to Brazil
  and the decision Open the Passports. Decisions such as Curb the Nobles'
  Privilege take back, a tier at a time, what the estates won during the
  move.
- **Next:** the Empire of Brazil, once you own Rio de Janeiro and no
  country ruled from Europe holds land in South America, and after it
  the New World.

## Notes

- **Two Crowns** needs 6 of the 8 royal offices in Rio, half your nobles
  and a third of your clergy in the Americas, and control in Portugal held
  at three quarters of its old level for 2 years. It leaves you the Regency
  Palace in Lisbon.
- **Portugal Let Go** releases Portugal as your subject. A revolt in
  Portugal forces it.
- **A failed move** sends the court back to Lisbon: 20 stability lost and
  no new attempt for 50 years. It happens if Rio falls, or after 50 years,
  unless you have already committed to Rio.
- Brazil's land grows no sugar, coffee or tobacco at the start. The
  Columbian exchange brings them.
