National Destinies gives formable countries in Europa Universalis V content of
their own. This page explains each part of it.

## Formation events

![The Birth of Spain event with the bonuses of one choice showing](img/formation_event_tall.jpg)

Forming a country opens an event about that moment in its history. It offers
two or three choices, and each grants a different bonus that lasts for a set
number of years. In most countries the choice also decides which destiny path
you follow.

## Advance trees

![Spain's own advances in the first age of the advance tree, one of them hovered](img/advance_tree.jpg)

Each country has advances of its own in the ages of the advance tree. They
give permanent bonuses and come from what the nation was known for, such as
its armies, its trade or its government. Some unlock a unit, a building or a
levy.

Where the base game already gives a country advances, they stay as they are
and the mod adds the ones that were missing.

## Destiny trees

![Castile's and Spain's destiny trees side by side in the advance tree](img/destiny_tree.jpg)

A destiny tree is a set of missions that opens after you form a country.

- The choice in the formation event opens one of two paths. Byzantium has
  three. A few countries, France among them, open their paths by what they do
  in the game.
- A path has three stages. A stage asks for things such as owning a named
  place, developing a named city, holding a building, gold, prestige or an
  army of a given size.
- Each stage gives a permanent bonus. Most paths end in a building that only
  that path unlocks.
- You keep a destiny tree when you go on to form another country, and you
  gain the new country's tree as well.

**Where to find it.** Open the advance tree and look in the Age of
Traditions, the first age. The destiny advances form a group of their own
among the other advances. The Danubian Monarchy is the exception, with stages
from the second age to the sixth.

![The game rule National Destinies Destiny Trees](img/rule_destiny_trees.jpg)

The game rule National Destinies Destiny Trees turns them off.

## Bureaucracies

![The list of institutions Spain can install](img/bureaucracy_list_tall.jpg)

Each country gains 4 to 8 historical institutions when it forms, such as a
royal mint, an examination board or a customs house. Each is a bureaucracy
you can install, with a funding level that scales its effects and its
drawbacks.

The mod also adds three doctrines and a law.

- **Three doctrines**, as government reforms. Patrimonial, Oligarchic and
  Meritocratic Doctrine exclude one another.
- **The Bureaucratic Funding law**, with four policies. Tax Farming, Crown
  Coffers, Estate Subvention and Treasury Bureau decide what installing and
  changing institutions costs. Treasury Bureau opens in the fifth age.

Byzantium and Trebizond get neither the doctrines nor the law, because the
base game already gives them bureaucracy content of their own.

![The game rule National Destinies Bureaucracies](img/rule_bureaucracies.jpg)

The game rule National Destinies Bureaucracies turns all of this off.

## Units, buildings and levies

![Spain's own advances as a list, two of them unlocking buildings](img/unique_advances_list_tall.jpg)

The mod adds unique units, buildings and levies where a country's history
has them. A country's advances unlock them, and so do some destiny paths.

## New formables

More than 50 countries in the mod do not exist in the base game, such as
Songhai, the Kilwa Sultanate, Great Tang and the Empire of Brazil. Each has the
same content as any other formable. In the Atlas, tick only **New** under
Type to list them.

To propose one, open a discussion on the
[Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/discussions/3668193813).
Countries that existed, or plausibly could have within the game's dates, are
added. Modern and fantasy countries are not.

## The Danubian Monarchy

![The Reaction of the Reich panel, with what the estrangement does and how it ends](img/reaction_of_the_reich_tall.jpg)

Austria, Hungary or Bohemia can form the Danubian Monarchy, the Habsburg
empire of all three crowns. It is the mod's largest country, with 90 events, two situations and
two disasters.

- **The Succession of the Danube** is a situation among the three crowns.
  Marriages, inheritance pacts and a congress decide which of them unites the
  others.
- **The Reaction of the Reich** starts if you leave the Holy Roman Empire.
  The Emperor gains a casus belli against you and other countries shun you.
  It ends when you beat the Emperor in a war, when he accepts a
  reconciliation, when the War of the Succession is over, or after 50 years
  with 60 legitimacy and 50 prestige.
- **The Nationalities Question** and **the War of the Succession** are
  disasters the Monarchy can suffer after it is founded.

The [Danubian Monarchy guide](index.html#DNM_f/guide) has the steps. Three game
rules turn the succession, the reaction and the disasters off one by one.

## Brazil

![The Transmigration panel, with the fleet, the royal offices and the ways the move can end](img/transmigration_panel.jpg)

Portugal can move its court to Rio de Janeiro, as it did in 1807, and go on
to become Brazil.

- The decision **Send the Court to Rio** makes Rio your capital and starts
  **the Transmigration**, a disaster with a panel of its own that runs the
  move. You have 50 years.
- A fleet sails every year. You choose what it carries, such as nobles and
  the court, clergy, merchants, settlers or supplies.
- You move the eight royal offices to Rio one at a time. Each one changes
  what you gain in Rio and who it upsets in Lisbon.
- A regency governs Portugal while the court is away, and you have to keep
  control there.
- With the Royal Council, the Supreme Court and the Royal Treasury in Rio,
  a quarter of your nobles and 15% of your clergy in the Americas, you can
  stay in Rio for good. After that the court cannot go back.
- The move ends with a return to Lisbon, with two crowns, or with Portugal
  let go. After either of the last two you can form Brazil.

The decision is in beta. Start from the [Portugal page](index.html#POR_f) or
read the [Brazil guide](index.html#BZL_f/guide).

## AI forms nations

The game rule AI Forms Nations has three settings. It is off by default.

![The game rule AI Forms Nations set to Ambitious](img/rule_ai_forms_nations.jpg)
![The game rule AI Formation Scope](img/rule_ai_formation_scope.jpg)

- **Off.** The base game's AI. It forms the nations it happens to qualify
  for and works toward none.
- **Opportunistic.** An AI country that meets the requirements of a major
  formable forms it. The nine are Spain, Russia, Great Britain, Scandinavia,
  the Mughals, the Danubian Monarchy, Germany, Italy and the Netherlands.
  How the AI fights its wars does not change.
- **Ambitious.** Every AI country works toward one formable, the one it
  formed in history or else the smallest it can reach. It wants that land,
  favours wars on the countries that hold it, forms the nation once it
  qualifies and then aims one step higher. The world is harder and changes
  more.

## Banks

The banks of the base game, such as the Peruzzi, the Bardi and the
Acciaioli, get mechanics of their own. This part of the mod is in beta.

- **The Money Market** shows a bank its debtors, its offices and its rival
  banks in one panel, with a map of who owes it.
- A bank lends, takes pledges for its loans and sells war bonds.
- Each bank has trust and greed. When trust fails, the bank faces a run.
- A bank can also hold land. It can pay for a war and take a town in the
  peace, seize a pledged place and take a country under its protection.

![The game rule National Destinies Banks](img/rule_banks.jpg)

The game rule National Destinies Banks has three settings. Full is all of
the above, Money Only leaves out everything about land, and Disabled keeps
banks as they are in the base game.

## Compatibility

- The mod is made for EU5 1.4. Across the Pillars is supported and not
  required.
- Start a new game to get everything. Added to a running save, the mod gives
  its advances, units and buildings, but a country already formed misses its
  formation event.
- A mod that changes the definitions of formable countries may conflict.
- If Steam keeps loading an old version, unsubscribe, clear the download
  cache in Steam's settings and subscribe again.
